# Get Out the Door

A cozy first-person hiking game that runs in your web browser. Hike the trails of six parks, camp under the stars, fish, cook over the fire, ride an ATV, a bike or a horse, buy a cabin in every park, and take the three-day Ridge to Sea Traverse from the forest, over the mountains, to the sea.

**Play it here: https://hankwhiteco.github.io/get-out-the-door/**

It needs a keyboard and mouse (best on a computer). Your progress is saved in your browser. **Settings → 💾 Save to a file** keeps a copy, and **📂 Load from a file** carries on from it on another computer.

## Controls

- **Mouse**: look around · **W A S D**: walk · **W twice** (and hold): run · **Shift**: walk carefully
- **E**: do things (sit, pitch your tent, sleep, shop, ride, read…)
- **Q**: eat a snack · **C**: cook at your campfire · **R**: roast a marshmallow
- **M**: map · **J**: journal · **I**: backpack · **O**: wardrobe · **1–9**: your tools
- **Esc**: pause

## Made with

Every sound, song and picture in the game is made with code: no recordings or image files. Built with [Three.js](https://threejs.org) and [Vite](https://vite.dev).

Made by **Great Bear Studios**.

## Running it on your computer

```
npm install
npm run dev
```

Then open the address it shows. `npm run build` makes the published version in `dist/`. Add `?test` to the address to try things without touching your save.

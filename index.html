    📱 Get Out the Door needs a keyboard and mouse, so it's best played on a computer.

    • Mouse look around
    • W A S D walk
    • ← → turn
    • Shift walk carefully (slower)
    • E sit, pitch tent, sleep, shop
    • Q eat a snack
    • Z your dog: sit and stay / whistle it back
    • B throw a stick for your dog
    • 1-9 pick a tool from your hotbar (or turn the mouse wheel)
    • H put away what you're holding
    • Space use · jump (horse) · spin (skates)
    • C cook at your campfire (or E at a stove)
    • R roast a marshmallow
    • T / Y radio on/off, next song
    • I backpack
    • J journal
    • M map
    • N sound on / off
    • G wave (with friends)
    • Enter chat (with friends)
    • V quick chat
    • O wardrobe (your look)
    • Esc pause
